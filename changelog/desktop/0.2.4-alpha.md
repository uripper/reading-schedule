# Changes in 0.2.4-alpha

## New

- Edit Book has a Remove book action; the book card keeps just Edit.
- The After Book field has a clear button, and its list shows each book's cover, title, and author.

## Improvements

- A fresh neo-brutalist look for every field: white boxes, black borders, and a hard shadow when focused instead of a glow.
- Menus open above dialogs and flip or shrink to stay inside the window, so they are never cut off at the edges.
- The Collections field shows chosen collections as chips inside it; the list keeps them with a check (choose again to remove), puts matches at the start of a word first, highlights what matched, and only creates a collection when you pick "Create" or "New collection…".
- After Book suggests books from the same shelf first, then books in the same collections, and never offers or saves a book that would make a loop.
- Book cards are calmer and line up across each row: no Collections line, progress only once a book is started, a shorter "After:" line, uncropped covers, a cleaner finish-date chip with the full date (like "Dec 3, 2026"), and new status icons.
- Edit Book: weekday toggles with checks, a Priority switch, a Difficulty stepper, a "min" suffix on minutes, thousands separators in Estimated Words (anything other than a whole number is flagged instead of changed), Clear only when a deadline is set, a full-size shelf checkbox, and Additional Options starts collapsed.
- The Library's Collections filter has an icon, and any active filter is highlighted.
